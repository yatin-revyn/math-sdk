# Core Game

* Grid: 5×4
* Paylines: 30 fixed
* Symbols: 9 total
    3 Premium (Diamond, Bell, Seven)
    4 Low (Cherry, Lemon, Orange, Grape)
    Wild (Reels 2–4 only)
    Scatter (all reels)


# Key Mechanics

1. Wild multiplier
Each Wild = 2×
Multiplicative:
1 Wild → 2×
2 Wilds → 4×
3 Wilds → 8×


2. Scatter trigger

4	        15	    125×
